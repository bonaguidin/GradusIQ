# How GradusIQ works (for the chat assistant)

Use this to answer questions about the GradusIQ app itself. Describe where
things are in plain words ("open Academic, then GPA Calculator"). If a
question is about a feature not described here, say you are not sure rather
than guessing. You cannot click, change or save anything for the student yet;
tell them where to do it.

## Layout

- The left sidebar has three sections: Overview, Academic and Career. Academic
  has GPA Calculator, Grade Calculator and Course Discovery under it. Career has
  Career Intelligence, Job Search and Career Profile under it.
- The top bar has a "?" button that replays the welcome tour, a button to
  report a bug or suggest a feature, and the profile menu.
- Overview shows the student's name, Official GPA, confirmed course count,
  profile status, degree progress ring, and this chat.

## Academic

- Academic (the section itself) shows the Academic Overview: Official and
  Projected GPA, current coursework, and "Coming up in Canvas" due dates.
- GPA Calculator: Official GPA, Projected GPA (from current grades in
  in-progress courses), a per-term GPA chart (click a term to open it), and a
  term planner to plan courses and try what-if grades.
- Grade Calculator: upload a course syllabus (PDF) and GradusIQ reads its
  grading breakdown, then shows the current course grade and what score is
  needed on remaining work to reach a target grade.
- Course Discovery: courses that fit the student's target roles, the degree
  requirement checklist, and the multi-year degree schedule builder. Degree
  requirement tracking only covers supported programs; for other programs it
  says so.

## Career

- Career Intelligence holds three analyses of the student's target roles:
  FIT (how well the profile matches each role), GAP (missing skills and next
  steps) and SHIFT (how the role is changing). Each starts with a "Run
  analysis" button; the latest result is saved and can be refreshed with
  "Re-run analysis".
- Job Search: live job postings for a chosen target role.
- Career Profile: target roles, technical and soft skills, work experience,
  projects and certifications. Students edit roles and skills here. Changing
  skills clears the saved FIT and GAP results so they can be re-run.

## Adding data

- Transcript: the "Upload transcript" page takes a PDF or Word (.docx)
  transcript up to 10 MB. Scanned images or photos without selectable text
  cannot be read yet. After upload the student reviews every course and grade,
  fixes anything wrong, and confirms. Only confirmed courses count toward GPA.
- Resume: the "Upload resume" page takes a PDF or Word (.docx) file up to
  10 MB (old .doc files must be re-saved as .docx). The student reviews the
  extracted experience, projects, skills and certifications, then confirms.
- Profile details: when an analysis is missing something (graduation date,
  majors, target roles and similar), GradusIQ asks for it on a short "complete
  your profile" step. These details can be changed later in Career Profile.
- Assistant lookups: when a question needs it, the assistant can check the
  student's degree plan, search the course catalog, read Canvas due dates
  further ahead, look at job postings, and read Grade Calculator grades
  (including the score needed on a remaining assignment). It only reads; it
  cannot change anything. A reply that used a lookup says "Checked ..." under it.
- Assistant memory: the assistant can offer to remember a lasting fact a
  student mentions (a goal or preference). Nothing is saved unless the student
  taps Remember. Saved notes are listed under "What GradusIQ remembers" in
  Career Profile, where any note can be deleted. Conversations themselves are
  kept only in the open browser tab.
- Canvas: on the Academic Overview, "Coming up in Canvas" connects the
  student's Canvas calendar. In Canvas, open Calendar, click "Calendar Feed"
  at the bottom right, copy the link and paste it into GradusIQ. This shows due
  dates and calendar events only, not Canvas grades. It refreshes every few
  hours on its own and has a Refresh button.

## Help

- Something broken or a feature idea: use the report button in the top bar.
- The welcome tour can be replayed from the "?" button.
